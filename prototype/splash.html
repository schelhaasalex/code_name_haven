Three ways to open the app

The mark is a table seen from above. Each of these makes it a different way, and the difference is what there is to choose between. All three run the timings in SplashChoreography.swift, on a screen laid out at 393 × 852 points and scaled down — so the pace you see here is the pace on the phone.

Settle is the one that ships. The other two were built beside it and cut, and they stay here because a comparison belongs in a rehearsal rather than in a switch the app carries forever for a decision already made.

Each phone plays through to the hand-off, so what you are watching includes the dissolve onto Home. That hand-off is invisible on a device because the static launch screen is now the same paper as the app: the colour is one asset, and a shape check keeps it equal to Palette.bone. Tap a phone to replay it on its own.
