How it keeps the rules

Rule 5Nothing to set up

The question has "Not now" and no follow-up. Without a reason, every line stays exactly as it reads today. The reason is a change in Settings, under "What it's for", never a step.

Rule 1Nobody else sees it

The reason lives in this phone's storage and the note is scheduled on this phone, so the server never holds it. A teenager's "Sleep" is not a parent's business, and a parent's "The kids" is not a teenager's. It never reaches the lock screen's Live Activity, which the room can see.

Rule 6The knowledge test

The app knows two things: what they said it was for, and when their phone was in the box. So it says "for the kids", never "with the kids", and never claims a chapter was read or a walk taken.

Rule 7No gate

Notification permission is asked only when someone taps "Yes, once a day", after an evening has already counted. Turning it down changes nothing else.

VoiceNo guilt

The reason is the one place guilt could get in. "The kids" is the answer this feature most invites, and the answer most easily turned into a weapon. Every note is an invitation in the present tense. None counts days, mentions a gap, or says who is waiting.

VisionUsed less, not more

The note stops by itself. Notes are scheduled a week ahead each time the app runs, so someone who stops opening the app gets a week of them and then silence. Nothing tries to win them back.

When the note comes

Each day gets at most one note. Its time is learned from the box and never asked for.

Its hourFifteen minutes before the median time their phone went in over the last fourteen days that counted, rounded to five minutes. On the first day, it is the time of that first evening, which is why the offer can say "around when your phone went in today" and be true.
NeverNever on a day that already counts. Never while the phone is in a box. Never between 10pm and 7am. Never more than one a day, and never one about ending an evening.
Taken backThe box wakes the app when a slot fills, through the same bluetooth-central connection it already uses, so today's pending note is removed the moment the phone goes in.
StoppingSeven days are scheduled ahead and refilled only when the app runs. No server push, no win-back. "No notes" in Settings clears them all.
SoundNone. A banner and a place in Notification Centre. A buzz that makes someone pick up their phone is the app becoming the problem.

Every line, by reason

Each reason is a few keys in copy/strings.json, so the words can change without a release. The note rotates through its lines, one per day, so it never reads like a machine repeating itself. The highlighted row is the reason picked above.

What it takes to build

No migration, no server change and no new dependency. One new pure type in ReclaimKit carries everything worth testing, and one shape check enforces the rule most likely to slip.

ReclaimKit/Support/Reason.swift       enum Reason: kids, eachOther, book, outside, sleep, making, quiet, own(String)
                                      stored in UserDefaults, never in Repository
ReclaimKit/Support/NoteSchedule.swift pure: (evening start times, today counts?, now) -> [Date]
                                      median − 15 min, rounded; skips counted days, 22:00–07:00
ReclaimKit/Tests/NoteScheduleTests    first day, median, counted day, quiet hours, seven-day horizon
Reclaim/Screens/ReasonSheet.swift     the question and the note offer, from the first receipt (screen 8)
Reclaim/Entry/DailyNote.swift         UNUserNotificationCenter: schedule 7, remove today's on "in"
copy/strings.json                     reason.*.label / .for / .home / .note[], reason.ask.*, note.offer.*
scripts/shape.sh                      the note is only scheduled by DailyNote, and only with
                                      interruptionLevel .passive or no sound; no reason key on the
                                      Live Activity

One thing to measure without reading anyone's reason: a write-only note per phone, "daily note on" or "off", and whether a box event followed a delivered note within two hours. That shows whether notes help without anyone learning what the time was for.
