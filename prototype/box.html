It leads with what it has become

The box ages the way a good object does: broken in at 7 days, settled in at 30, seasoned at 100, part of the house at 365. Before the first it's just on its way, with no age or count claimed. A day is one any phone did fifteen minutes in, so one person on their own moves it exactly as a family does. Only ever forward, and there's no "16 to go".

The figures sit under it

Hours together and days, large. "Since September." moves up under the name (the existing place.stage.since), because it's part of the box's story, not a statistic.

The radio is a status, not a sentence

A dot (filled when connected, hollow when away, clay when Bluetooth is off) and the existing sentence, with its fix underneath when there is one. It moves above the name, because a box that can't hear your phone matters more than what it's called.

The name keeps its field

Same pencil and same save behaviour, moved down: you name a box once. When someone else named it, the field becomes a dashed outline with "Named by whoever first scanned it."

Forget has an icon

It matches Delete on Settings: quiet, with the weight in the confirmation.

Everything here is the box's

Everyone's days at it added up, and nothing about who. Same aggregate the page already reads (summary(of:)), so the page needs no new call.
