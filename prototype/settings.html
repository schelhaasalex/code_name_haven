How it works

Put your phone in a slot and it begins.

It counts from fifteen minutes in.

Take it out and it ends, unless it's back within five seconds.

Left in overnight, we close it at three hours. It still counts.

What we can see

When your phone goes into a box and comes out, and which box, and when setting up or the box goes wrong, so it can be put right. Nothing about your screen, your apps, or where you are.

Everyone at a box sees how many of you are in it, and what it has kept, added up. Never who wasn't there.

The box leads with what it has kept

Its name, its radio as a dot, and underneath, its totals: the ones its own page already shows (box.kept.*), added up, so nothing new crosses a person boundary.

Rules as a timeline

"In · 15 min · 5 sec · 3 hrs" is how the evening actually runs. The same four sentences, hung on a line, so the numbers can be found at a glance. Still facts, still no dials — a house tuning its own minimum is setup by another name.

Privacy is a link

What we can see belongs in a privacy policy, not spelled out on this page. One row, opening the policy on LINK_DOMAIN, and that page doesn't exist yet.

Your days says what it is

Kept: a sealed mark and one line. Not kept: the offer, in a card, with the Apple button inside it — offered, never asked anywhere else.

The ring problem leads

When the lock-screen ring is off it's the only thing on this page that needs a person to act, so it goes first, in clay, with its fix.

A quiet foot

Delete your account, with an icon so it reads as an action rather than a caption. No Sign out: an account that is only an Apple ID link has nothing to sign out to. The weight stays in the confirmation.
